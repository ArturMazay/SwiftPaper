## Full description

```
SwiftPaper is a fast PDF scanner and document toolkit for Android. Scan papers with your camera, turn photos into PDF, compress large files, and merge PDFs — all processed on your device.

◆ PDF SCANNER
Capture documents with the camera and export clean multi-page PDFs. Ideal for receipts, contracts, notes, and forms.

◆ IMAGE TO PDF
Convert one or many images into a single PDF. Pick from gallery or camera, reorder pages, and export when ready.

◆ COMPRESS PDF
Shrink oversized PDFs for email, messaging, or uploads without leaving the app. Choose a quality that fits your needs.

◆ MERGE PDF
Combine multiple PDF files into one document in seconds. Perfect for bundling reports, scans, and signed pages.

◆ ON-DEVICE & PRIVATE
Your files stay on your phone. SwiftPaper processes documents locally — no account required to scan, convert, compress, or merge.

◆ EXPORT & SHARE
Save results and share via any app. Recent jobs appear in History so you can reopen or re-export quickly.

◆ SWIFT PAPER PRO (OPTIONAL)
One-time unlock: remove ads, HD exports without watermark, and batch / unlimited merges. No subscription.

Keywords people search: pdf scanner, image to pdf, compress pdf, merge pdf, document scanner — SwiftPaper covers them in one simple home screen.

Download SwiftPaper and get from photo to PDF in seconds.
```
