## 1. Overview
Welcome to **SwiftPaper** ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles information when you use our document scanning, image conversion, PDF compression, and PDF merging services.

**Key Principle:** SwiftPaper processes your documents **100% locally on your device**. We do not collect, store, or upload your photos, scanned documents, or generated PDFs to any external servers.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Local Device Data (Processed Locally Only)
- **Camera Data:** Used solely to capture physical documents, receipts, and notes when you use the Scanner feature.
- **Photos & Media Files:** Used solely to let you select images from your device gallery to convert them into PDF files or compress them.
- **Generated PDF Files:** Saved directly to your device’s local storage or app-specific storage.

*None of your photos, camera frames, or PDF files leave your device.*

### B. Automatically Collected Technical Data (Third-Party Services)
To provide free features and in-app purchases, SwiftPaper integrates standard third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs):

1. **Google Play Billing:**
   - Handles secure in-app purchases and subscriptions (e.g., SwiftPaper Pro).
   - We do not store or process your credit card or financial details. All transactions are handled securely by Google Play.
   - [Google Play Services Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

2. **Yandex Mobile Ads SDK:**
   - Displays banner ads to support free usage of the App.
   - May collect anonymous technical identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID, IP address, device model, system language) to serve relevant ads.
   - [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.com/legal/privacy/)

---

## 3. App Permissions and Why We Need Them

To perform its core functions, SwiftPaper requests the following Android permissions:

- **`android.permission.CAMERA`**: Required to capture document pages using the camera scanner.
- **`android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` / Media Permissions (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`)**: Required to pick photos from your gallery for PDF conversion.
- **`android.permission.INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`**: Required to load advertisement banners and verify in-app purchase status via Google Play.

You can grant or revoke these permissions at any time through your device's Settings.

---

## 4. Data Retention and Security
- **No Cloud Storage:** We do not maintain any cloud databases or user accounts.
- **Local Control:** All created, scanned, and compressed documents remain under your full control in your device memory. Deleting the App or clearing app data removes locally cached files.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy
SwiftPaper is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through third-party services, please contact us.

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
Depending on your region, you have rights regarding your personal data:
- Right to revoke camera and media access via device Settings.
- Right to reset your Advertising ID via device Settings (`Settings > Google > Ads`).
- Right to request information on third-party SDK data processing by contacting us.
