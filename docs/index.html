SwiftPaper Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 8, 2026 | App: SwiftPaper: PDF Scanner & Editor
🔒 Key Principle: SwiftPaper processes all your scanned documents, photos, and PDF files 100% locally on your device. Your personal files are never uploaded to external servers.

1. Overview

Welcome to SwiftPaper ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your data. This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles information when you use our document scanning, photo-to-PDF conversion, file compression, and PDF merging features.

2. Information We Collect and Process

A. Local Device Data (Processed 100% Locally)

  • Camera Data: Used solely to capture document pages when you use the Scanner feature.
  • Photos & Media: Used solely to let you select images from your gallery to convert into PDFs or compress.
  • Generated PDF Files: Saved directly to your device's local storage.

B. Automatically Collected Data (Third-Party SDKs)

To provide free features and in-app purchase functionality, SwiftPaper uses standard third-party services:

  • Google Play Billing: Handles secure in-app purchases (e.g., SwiftPaper Pro). Financial details are processed securely by Google. Google Privacy Policy
  • Yandex Mobile Ads SDK: Displays advertisement banners. May collect anonymous technical identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID, IP address, device model) to serve relevant ads. Yandex Privacy Policy

3. App Permissions

SwiftPaper requests the following Android permissions:

  • CAMERA: To capture document pages.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_IMAGES: To import photos from gallery.
  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: To show ads and verify Pro purchases via Google Play.

4. Data Retention & Security

We do not maintain any cloud databases or user accounts. All documents remain stored on your physical device under your full control. Clearing app data or uninstalling the app deletes locally cached files.
